## Platform

web

## Users

Local people in Metairie and Greater New Orleans with a broken or slow phone,
tablet, or computer. They want to know fast whether this shop fixes their
device, where it is, and whether it is open now. Small businesses and homeowners
use the networking and on-site services.

## Product Purpose

PC Tune-Ups is a walk-in device repair shop. The landing page exists to turn a
local search into a store visit. Success means the visitor gets directions and
walks in during open hours. Calling the shop is the secondary action.

## Positioning

A neighborhood repair counter with 10 years in business on Veterans Blvd. One
shop covers computers, Apple devices, data recovery, networking, and custom builds.

## Product Principles

1. Location, hours, and directions come first. The visit is the goal.
2. Answer "do you fix my device?" in one glance.
3. Show only real proof: real photos, real reviews, real tenure.
